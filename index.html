OUR POLICY

Return & Replacement Policy

We want every order from SHEETAL BAKERY & LIVE CAKE SHOP to reach you fresh, correct and in perfect condition.

🔄

Return / Replacement

If you receive a wrong, damaged, defective or spoiled product, please contact us as soon as possible. Eligible items may be replaced or refunded after verification.

🍰

Fresh Cakes & Bakery Items

Fresh cakes, bakery products and other perishable food items cannot normally be returned after delivery because of their freshness and food-safety requirements.

📦

Wrong Item Received

If you receive an item different from what you ordered, please contact us immediately. We will verify the order and arrange a suitable replacement or refund.

📸

Proof of Issue

For damaged, wrong or defective products, please provide clear photos or videos of the product and packaging. This helps us process your request quickly.

💰

Refund

If a refund is approved, the refund amount and method will be communicated to you after verification of the issue and order details.

Important: Return, replacement or refund requests are subject to verification. Custom-made cakes and personalized products may have additional conditions and cannot normally be cancelled or returned once preparation has started.

Need Help?

For return, replacement or refund assistance, please contact SHEETAL BAKERY & LIVE CAKE SHOP through WhatsApp or phone.
